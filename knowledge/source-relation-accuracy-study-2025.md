## Citation

Chen, L., Park, S., & Rodriguez, M. (2025). "Typed Relations and Answer Accuracy in Agent Systems." *Proceedings of the Workshop on Knowledge-Augmented Agents*, ACL 2025.

## Key Findings

- 127% improvement in answer precision when agents use typed relations vs. untyped edges
- Relation types reduce search space by 40-65% in multi-hop queries
- Agents make fewer incorrect tool selections when relation types are semantically meaningful
- Most effective relation types: SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, DERIVED_FROM
