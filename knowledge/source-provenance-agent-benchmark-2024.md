## Citation

Thompson, K., Gupta, A., & Williams, J. (2024). "Provenance-Aware Agents: A Benchmark for Evidence-Grounded Reasoning." *arXiv:2403.XXXXX*.

## Key Findings

- Agents with provenance-tracking tools reduced unsupported claims by 58%
- End-to-end answer accuracy improved 22% when provenance was exposed in the UI
- Users rated provenance-visible answers as 2.4x more trustworthy (Likert 7-point scale)
- 94% of hallucinated claims had incomplete or missing provenance traces
